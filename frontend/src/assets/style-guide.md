# Coffee Journal Style Guide

_Source reference_: `codex/coffee_journal/style_example.jpg` (dark café table + tasting notebook).

## Palette
- **Night Roast** `#1B1614` — primary background / full-bleed sections
- **Espresso Bark** `#3B2924` — nav bars, cards shadow edges
- **Crema Paper** `#F8F1E8` — foreground cards and text backgrounds
- **Caramel Stitch** `#C89C73` — dividers, CTAs, accent borders
- **Moss Ink** `#56645D` — muted body copy and journal lines
- **Amber Ember** `#A8563C` — rating pills, interactive highlights

## Typography
- **Headings**: `Cormorant Garamond` (serif, 500 weight) for artisan notebook feel
- **Body & UI**: `Inter` (sans) for crisp readability on data tables & forms
- **Numeric / Data**: `Space Mono` fallback for telemetry readouts (optional)

## Components
- **Cards**: Off-white paper with subtle grain, rounded 18px corners, thin caramel borders, drop shadow `0 20px 80px rgba(0,0,0,0.45)`
- **Separators**: Dotted/dashed horizontal rules to mimic lined journal pages
- **Buttons**: Filled espresso background, caramel text; outlined variant with crema background
- **Inputs**: Transparent espresso surfaces with crema text, lined background for grouped controls

## Charts & Data Viz
- Trend lines: Amber stroke with circle markers, fill gradient Night→Transparent
- Bar charts: Crema bars with espresso outlines, active bar tinted Amber
- Flavor tags: pill badges with Moss text + thin caramel borders

Use this guide to keep the dark café aesthetic consistent across new surfaces.
